Frequently asked questions

Is the consultation really free?

Yes. The first conversation is free. We look at your situation and I tell you which path fits you.

Do you guarantee the visa?

No. Only immigration issues visas. I help prepare your documents properly and reduce the risk of refusal.

What if my visa was refused before?

In many cases you can apply again. First we look at the reason for the refusal together, then plan the next step.

Can I apply without knowing Korean?

Yes. Many students start with a Korean language course. In the consultation we work out which path fits you.
